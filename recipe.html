Beef Enchiladas

Beef enchiladas have roots in ancient Mesoamerican traditions of filling corn tortillas with ingredients like fish or beans. Their modern form developed after the Spanish introduced beef, cheese, and chili sauces. The name "enchilada" comes from the Spanish verb “enchilar”, meaning to add chili.

This beef enchilada recipe combines seasoned ground beef, melted cheese, and flavorful enchilada sauce wrapped in warm tortillas. It is a comforting dish perfect for family dinners and gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
    • Lean beef works well
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cheese
  • 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook ground beef and onions, then:
    1. Drain excess fat.
    2. Season with chili powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper.
  3. Warm tortillas until soft.
  4. Fill each tortilla with beef mixture and cheese; roll tightly.
  5. Place in baking dish, cover with enchilada sauce.
  6. Top with cheese and bake 20 minutes.
  7. Serve warm.
