Beef Stew

Beef stew is a hearty, protein-rich comfort food with ancient roots, evolving from early slow-cooked meat and vegetable pots to the French ragout and modern variations.

It provides iron, B vitamins, zinc, and satisfying warmth, often made with less tender cuts of beef that become tender through long simmering in flavorful broth, making it a resourceful and nutritious meal perfect for leftovers.
