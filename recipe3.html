Recipe For Beef Stew

About Beef stew

Beef stew is a hearty, protein-rich comfort food with ancient roots, evolving from early slow-cooked meat and vegetable pots to the French ragout and modern variations, providing iron, B vitamins, zinc, and satisfying warmth, often made with less tender cuts of beef that become tender through long simmering in flavorful broth, making it a resourceful and nutritious meal perfect for leftovers.

Ingredients used in beef stew.

Classic beef stew ingredients include beef (like chuck roast), vegetables (onions, carrots, potatoes, celery, garlic), liquids (beef broth, sometimes red wine), thickeners (flour, cornstarch), and seasonings (bay leaves, thyme, salt, pepper, Worcestershire sauce)

The recipe to make beef stew.

  1. Brown the beef: Season the beef cubes generously with salt and pepper, then toss them in the flour to coat.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the beef in batches, making sure not to overcrowd the pan, and brown well on all sides. Remove the browned beef and set it aside.
  3. Sauté aromatics: Reduce the heat to medium. In the same pot, add the chopped onion and cook until softened and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  4. Build the sauce: Stir in the tomato paste, carrots, potatoes, and thyme, mixing well to coat the vegetables. Pour in the beef broth and red wine (if using), using a wooden spoon to scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot (deglazing). Stir in the Worcestershire sauce and add the bay leaves.
  5. Simmer: Return the beef and any accumulated juices to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover the pot and simmer gently for 1.5 to 2 hours, or until the beef is very tender and yields easily to a fork.

Enjoy!
